Who We Are

Discover BlackTerminal Computer Institute.

BlackTerminal Computer Institute is a computer education and skill development institute based in Baripada, Odisha.

Our goal is to help students develop practical computer knowledge, improve their technical skills and become confident in the digital world.

We focus on learning, practice, problem solving and continuous improvement so that students can build valuable skills for their future.
